Kept deliberately general here (naming that Nuh is approached early, that Muhammad ﷺ alone is granted full intercession) rather than reproducing the detailed dialogue attributed to each prophet in the full hadith, since that level of specific wording deserves more care than a one-paragraph reference allows.
