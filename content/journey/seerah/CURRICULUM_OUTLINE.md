# The Long Road to Madinah — Full 365-Day Curriculum Outline

Reference outline for the Seerah/Qisas al-Anbiya journey track (see content/journey/seerah/README.md). Generated from the researched blueprint; day content is authored progressively into content/journey/seerah/day-NNN/.

### Era 00 — The First Religion (Days 1–10)

Before history as we usually tell it: the Oneness of Allah declared before creation, the first human made khalīfah, the first fall and the first forgiveness — establishing the one truth every prophet after this will simply renew.

| Day | Title | Anchor | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | Before Creation | Q 112:1–4 | Al-Ikhlāṣ states the truth that predates the universe: Allah is One, needing nothing, resembling nothing. |
| 002 | The Covenant of Every Soul | Q 7:172 | Before any of us were born, every soul testified: "Am I not your Lord?" — "Yes, we testify." |
| 003 | The Creation of Adam | Q 2:30–33 | Angels question, Allah teaches Adam the names of all things, and khilāfah — stewardship of the earth — is announced. |
| 004 | Iblīs Refuses to Bow | Q 7:11–18 | Pride, not ignorance, is the root of the first rebellion — and the origin of open enmity toward humankind. |
| 005 | Adam and Ḥawwā' in the Garden | Q 2:35–36, 7:19–22 | One tree, one instruction, and the whisper that made it feel bigger than it was. |
| 006 | The First Tawbah | Q 2:37, 7:23 | "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves" — the words that became every believer's return. |
| 007 | Sent Down With a Promise | Q 20:123–124 | "Whoever follows My guidance will not go astray nor suffer" — guidance promised before the first footstep on earth. |
| 008 | Hābīl and Qābīl | Q 5:27–32 | The first murder, the first grief, and the raven that taught a killer how to bury his brother — the range extends to 5:32 to include the "saved all mankind" verse the story itself draws as its lesson. |
| 009 | Idrīs, the Raised One | Q 19:56–57, 21:85 | Truthful, patient, and raised to "a high station" — one of the earliest names in the chain of prophethood. |
| 010 | One Message, Many Messengers | Q 21:25, 16:36 | "We did not send any messenger before you except that We revealed to him: there is no god but Me" — the thread that ties every day that follows to this first one. |

### Era 01 — Prophets Before Ibrahim (Days 11–30)

Nūḥ's nine hundred and fifty years of calling, Hūd and Ṣāliḥ confronting nations proud of their own strength, and Lūṭ standing against a city that had lost its shame — the recurring shape of a people forgetting, and mercy that keeps calling anyway.

### Era 02 — Ibrahim, Father of Prophets (Days 31–60)

From an idol-carver's household to "Khalīlullāh," the Friend of Allah: the fire that would not burn him, the wife and infant son left in an empty valley on trust alone, the well that answered that trust, the House they raised together, and the sacrifice that gave the whole Ummah a annual reminder of submission.

### Era 03 — Yusuf — The Best of Stories (Days 61–75)

Sūrah Yūsuf calls itself "the best of stories," and it is told here exactly as it unfolds in the Qur'an: one continuous narrative of betrayal, slavery, false accusation, imprisonment, power, and a forgiveness so complete it closes every wound it opens.

### Era 04 — Musa and Bani Isra'il (Days 76–110)

No prophet is mentioned more often in the Qur'an than Mūsā. A palace-raised fugitive becomes the one who confronts the most powerful man on earth — and then spends the rest of his life patiently, exhaustedly, mercifully leading a nation that kept forgetting what it had just seen.

A shepherd boy who kills a giant becomes a prophet-king whose voice the mountains themselves answer in worship, and a son who commands wind and jinn learns that gratitude, not power, is what a throne is actually for.

### Era 07 — Jahiliyyah: The World Waiting (Days 151–165)

A long gap without a new messenger, and Arabia drifts: three hundred sixty idols around a House built for One God, buried daughters, tribal pride — while two empires spend themselves in endless war, unaware that the next revelation is about to arrive in the last place either expected.
