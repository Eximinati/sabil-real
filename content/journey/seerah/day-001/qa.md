# Day 1 Bilingual Emotional QA

## Emotional equivalence
- Both languages open with stillness before any explanation.
- Urdu keeps the same settling, unhurried tone rather than a formal theological register.

## Reflection safety
- Prompt is descriptive/curious, not a test of correct belief.

## Tiny action tone
- A single sincere phrase, not a checklist.

## Seerah/Hadith accuracy
- Hadith reference (Surah Al-Ikhlas equal to a third of the Qur'an) is a well-established, widely authenticated narration; no specific collection/number cited to avoid misattribution risk.

## Mobile readability
- Short paragraphs, verse list kept spacious.
