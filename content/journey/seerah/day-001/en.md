# Day 1 - Before Anything Existed

## Arrival moment

Take one quiet breath.
Before the first star, before the first sky, before you — Allah simply is.

## Opening reflection

Everything you have ever known had a beginning. Every friend, every fear, every sunrise.
Only One does not. Before creation existed to praise Him, He was already worthy of all praise.
This is where every true religion begins, and where this whole year-long journey begins too: not with a rule, not with a ritual, but with a single, settling truth.

## Seerah moment

Centuries later, this same truth would be given to a Prophet in four short verses outside Makkah.
He loved this chapter so much that he told his companions reciting it once carries the weight of a third of the entire Qur'an.
Not because it is long. Because it says the one thing that matters most, as simply as it can be said.

## Quran reflection

Read these four verses slowly, as if hearing them for the first time.

112:1
112:2
112:3
112:4

## Tafsir support

"Say: He is Allah, One." Not "a god" among others — the only reality of that kind.
"As-Samad" — the One depended upon, who depends on nothing.
No parent, no child, no equal. Every comparison you could reach for falls away.
This is not a philosophy lesson. It is the ground you are about to stand on for the next 365 days.

## Hadith connection

The Prophet ﷺ told his companions that this short surah is equal, in reward, to a third of the Qur'an. What is said in four lines is enough to reorient an entire life.

## Private reflection

When you picture "God" in your mind, what do you actually picture? Where did that picture come from — and does it match the One described here?

## Tiny action

Say "Allahu Ahad" — Allah is One — slowly, once, meaning every word, before you sleep tonight.

## Closing moment

You did not begin this truth, and you will not end it. You are only, gently, returning to it.
Return tomorrow. The story is only just beginning.
