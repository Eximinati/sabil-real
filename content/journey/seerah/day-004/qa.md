# Day 4 Bilingual Emotional QA

## Emotional equivalence
- Frames pride as a pattern to recognize in oneself, not a lecture about Iblis as a distant villain — kept consistent in both languages.

## Seerah/Hadith accuracy
- Hadith (no atom's weight of pride enters Paradise; pride defined as rejecting truth and belittling people) is Sahih Muslim 91, a well-known and precisely quotable narration.

## Reflection safety
- Prompt targets a common, relatable self-justification pattern rather than asking the reader to confess a specific sin.
