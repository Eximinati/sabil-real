# Day 2 - A Promise You Already Made

## Arrival moment

Before you take your next breath, know this: this is not the first time you have recognized your Lord.

## Opening reflection

Sometimes faith feels like learning something brand new — memorizing a fact you didn't know yesterday.
But the Qur'an describes something different. It describes remembering.

## Seerah moment

The Prophet ﷺ taught his companions about the fitrah — the natural recognition of Allah placed inside every human being.
He said every child is born upon this fitrah; it is only what happens afterward that can cover it over.
You were not talked into believing in one God. Somewhere beneath everything you were taught later, you already knew.

## Quran reflection

Sit with the moment every soul was asked a single question.

7:172

## Tafsir support

Before any of us walked the earth, every soul was gathered and asked: "Am I not your Lord?"
And every soul answered: "Yes, we testify."
Scholars describe this not as a memory you can consciously recall, but as something written into you — why the sky can still catch your breath, why silence can still feel like an invitation.

## Hadith connection

"Every child is born upon the fitrah" — a well-known teaching of the Prophet ﷺ (Bukhari), explaining that recognition of Allah is not something added to you from outside, but something uncovered.

## Private reflection

Has there been a moment — in nature, in grief, in stillness — where believing in Allah felt less like a decision and more like recognizing something you already knew?

## Tiny action

Today, when something quietly moves you — a sky, a kindness, a relief — pause and say silently: "I know You."

## Closing moment

You are not being asked to believe in a stranger. You are being invited to remember. Return tomorrow, gently.
