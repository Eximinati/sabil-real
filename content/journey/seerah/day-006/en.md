# Day 6 - The Words That Became Every Return

## Arrival moment

Whatever you are carrying right now that you haven't said out loud to Allah — today has room for it.

## Opening reflection

After the tree, after the shame, Adam and Hawwa did not run from Allah. They turned toward Him.

## Seerah moment

Their words are recorded exactly, becoming the first tawbah in human history — a template every sincere return has followed since: "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."
No excuses offered. No blame passed to Shaytan, though he was there. Just honesty.

## Quran reflection

Read the words that turned a fall into a forgiven fall.

2:37
7:23

## Tafsir support

Allah didn't just forgive them — the ayah says Allah "received" these words from Adam, as if the du'a itself was a gift returning to its Giver.
This is what separates a mistake from a wound that never heals: not whether you fell, but whether you turned back afterward.

## Private reflection

If you spoke to Allah tonight with the same honesty Adam did — no excuses, no blame passed elsewhere — what would your own sentence be?

## Tiny action

Say quietly, in your own words: "My Lord, I have wronged myself." Then simply stop, and let it be enough for tonight.

## Closing moment

This is not the story of a perfect first man. It is the story of the first return — and every return since has followed his lead. Return tomorrow, gently.
