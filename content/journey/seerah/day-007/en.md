# Day 7 - Not Abandoned on Earth

## Arrival moment

Whatever "earth" feels like to you today — overwhelming, lonely, ordinary — hear this first: you were not sent here without a promise.

## Opening reflection

When Adam and Hawwa left the garden, it could have felt like exile with nothing offered in return. It wasn't.

## Seerah moment

In the very same breath as sending them down, Allah makes a promise that will hold for every human being who ever lives: guidance is coming, and whoever follows it will not go astray, nor suffer.
Not "some of you." Not "if you're good enough first." Whoever follows it.

## Quran reflection

Read this as a promise made directly to you, not just to Adam.

20:123
20:124

## Tafsir support

Two paths are named here, plainly: follow the guidance and be safe from both misguidance and misery, or turn away from it and live a "straitened life" — not necessarily poor, but narrow, restless, never quite settled.
This is Allah's first word about what life on earth would actually be like: hard, but never without a way through.

## Private reflection

Where in your life right now does it feel "straitened" — narrow, restless? What guidance have you been putting off following?

## Tiny action

Pick one piece of guidance you already know but haven't been living — even something small — and take one step toward it today.

## Closing moment

You were never sent to earth to figure it out alone. Return tomorrow, gently.
