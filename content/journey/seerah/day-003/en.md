# Day 3 - You Are Not an Accident

## Arrival moment

Breathe in. Somewhere in that breath is an answer to a question you may never have asked out loud: why am I here?

## Opening reflection

Long before your birth, before your parents, before this planet ever felt human feet, Allah spoke about you to the angels.

## Seerah moment

This is not a distant myth. The Qur'an describes an actual, deliberate announcement: Allah tells the angels He is placing a khalifah — a trusted steward — on the earth.
The angels ask a fair question: will this being cause corruption and bloodshed?
Allah's answer is not an argument. It is simply: "I know what you do not know."

## Quran reflection

Read slowly. This is Allah introducing humanity to itself for the first time.

## Tafsir support

Allah teaches Adam the names of all things — a capacity for knowledge given to no other creation in this way.
When the angels cannot answer, Adam can. Not because he is greater than angels in worship, but because he carries something they don't: the capacity to learn, to choose, to grow.
That capacity is still inside you.

## Private reflection

"I know what you do not know," Allah said, about placing you here. What might Allah know about your life right now that you can't yet see?

## Tiny action

Write one honest sentence today that begins: "I am here because—" Let it be true, not polished.

## Closing moment

You were announced before you were born. You are not a mistake that happened to the world. Return tomorrow, gently.
