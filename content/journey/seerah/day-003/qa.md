# Day 3 Bilingual Emotional QA

## Emotional equivalence
- "You are not an accident" / purpose framing preserved in both languages without becoming a lecture on angels vs. humans.

## Hadith connection intentionally omitted
- The best-known hadith directly about Adam's creation ("Allah created Adam in His image") carries real theological sensitivity (anthropomorphism discussions) unsuited to a beginner-safe devotional context. Rather than force a citation, this day rests on the ayat alone.

## Reflection safety
- Prompt is open and identity-affirming, not a demand for certainty about one's purpose.
