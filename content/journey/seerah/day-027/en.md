# Day 27 - A Host's Fear for His Guests

## Arrival moment

Notice today how quickly hospitality turns to fear when the world outside a home has gone this wrong.

## Opening reflection

Angels arrived at Lut's home in human form, the same messengers who had just brought Ibrahim news of a son.

## Seerah moment

Lut's reaction wasn't relief at guests. It was dread: "This is a difficult day." He knew exactly what his city would do if they learned of these visitors, and he had no way yet to explain that his guests could defend themselves.
His wife, quietly, was the one who alerted the city outside — a detail the Qur'an will return to.

## Tafsir support

The angels reassure Lut: "we will surely save you and your family, all except your wife; she is to be of those who remain behind." Even inside his own household, belief — not proximity — determined who would be saved.
Lut is told to leave in the depths of the night, and not to look back — a command about trusting the way forward rather than lingering over what's being left.

## Private reflection

Is there a "looking back" you've been doing — toward a past sin, a past place, a past version of yourself — that you need Allah's permission to simply stop doing?

## Tiny action

Choose one thing from your past you keep looking back at, and consciously turn your attention forward today instead.

## Closing moment

Lut was told to walk forward and not look back. That instruction is being offered to you too, right now. Return tomorrow.
