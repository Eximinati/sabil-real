# Day 5 - One Tree, One Whisper

## Arrival moment

This is not a story about how bad humans are. It's a story about how ordinary a first mistake can look.

## Opening reflection

Adam and Hawwa were given an entire garden, with only one boundary: don't come near this one tree.

## Seerah moment

Shaytan didn't tempt them with something ugly. He whispered that the tree might make them angels, or grant them immortality — dressing disobedience up as opportunity, almost as a favor.
That is worth sitting with: the pull toward wrong rarely looks like wrong while it's happening.

## Tafsir support

They were not tricked into hating Allah. They were tricked into forgetting, for a moment, what they already knew.
The moment the fruit was tasted, what they felt first wasn't punishment from outside — it was shame from inside, immediate and unmistakable.

## Private reflection

Think of the last time you knew something wasn't right, and did it anyway. What did the "whisper" sound like, in your own words?

## Tiny action

Name, honestly, one boundary in your life right now that you keep circling closer to. Just name it — nothing more today.

## Closing moment

Being tempted is not the end of the story. What comes next is. Return tomorrow, gently.
